# 🚀 Zainotes v2.1.0

Welcome to **Zainotes v2.1.0**! This release introduces rich multimedia note attachments, customizable theme accent color palettes, AI smart session titles, and refined Material 3 Expressive UI components. ✨

---

## ⚡ What is New in Version 2.1.0

### 📸 1. Embedded Media Attachments
- 🖼️ **Image Blocks**: Embed photos and pictures with high-resolution rendering, rounded corner framing, and full-screen preview.
- 🎙️ **Audio Blocks**: Attach voice notes and audio clips with an interactive waveform player, play/pause controls, and duration scrubbing slider.
- 🎬 **Video Blocks**: Attach video files with integrated playback and full-screen viewing support.
- 💾 **Persistent Internal Storage**: Media attachments are safely copied and indexed in the app private internal storage to prevent broken links.

### 🎨 2. Theme Accent Color Palettes
- 🌈 Choose from 8 handcrafted accent palettes when Dynamic Wallpaper Color (Monet) is disabled:
  - 🟣 Purple
  - 🌊 Ocean Blue
  - 🌿 Emerald
  - 🌸 Rose Pink
  - 🌅 Sunset Gold
  - 🔴 Coral Red
  - 🩵 Aqua Teal
  - 🔷 Indigo
- 🖤 Seamlessly adapts across Light and pure `#000000` AMOLED modes.

### 🤖 3. Smart AI Chat Session Titles
- 🏷️ Automatic generation of concise, descriptive topic titles for AI chat threads based on conversation content.
- 🧵 Enhanced multi-session chat drawer with quick switching, editing, and persistent history.
- ⚡ Improved streaming responses and action approvals for note drafts.

### 🛡️ 4. Full R8 Optimization and In-Place Updates
- 📦 Full R8 minification and resource shrinking applied for minimum APK size and blazing launch speeds.
- 🔑 Signed with the permanent repository release keystore valid until **2054**.
- 📲 Seamless in-place upgrade from v1.2 and v2.0 without uninstalling.
